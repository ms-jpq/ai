# Terminology Distillation

## Detect

- Identify a recurring meaning, relation, or distinction that currently needs a long or unstable description.

- Collect its uses, counterexamples, and neighboring terms.

## Distill

- Propose one term with a precise boundary and a short definition.

- Preserve distinctions that matter to a decision or verification.

- Reject a term that merges different meanings only to make prose shorter.

## Evolve

- Introduce, revise, merge, or retire terms as the situation model changes.

- Update the affected local context consistently after a term is confirmed.

- Keep unresolved aliases explicit rather than silently collapsing them.

# Semantic Engineering

## Isolate

- Identify a concept, distinction, relation, or constraint that is ambiguous, unstable, or represented inconsistently.

## Baseline

- Collect its uses, counterexamples, neighboring concepts, and representations.

## Engineer

- Define the concept before choosing or revising its name.

  - State what belongs, what does not, its relations, and which distinctions must survive.

- Align its definition, constraint, and short name across each relevant representation.

## Falsify

- Reject a definition or name that is mere decoration, merges concepts, or loses a necessary distinction.

- Test the resulting semantics against collected uses, counterexamples, and neighboring concepts.

## Revise

- Reuse confirmed definitions and names across code, schemas, diagrams, documents, and conversation.

- Keep unresolved aliases explicit rather than silently collapsing them.

- Revise or retire a definition or name when later evidence changes its grounded distinction.

The result is a ubiquitous language: precise names and definitions that remain aligned across each relevant representation of the domain.

# Refine

## Artifact (Output)

_State the intended properties declaratively._

- **Prose:** Clear wording and structure serving the reader's goal without losing meaning.

---

## Observation (Input)

_State the input categories and their roles._

- **Prose:** The text and context to revise.

- **Reader:** Their goal, knowledge, and vocabulary.

---

## Abduction

_Devise an explanation for observations._

- Select one reader-facing defect per pass.

- Explain how the wording or structure causes the defect, then propose a revision.

- Break prose into point form.

  - One claim per bullet.

- Replace vague words with precise ones. Remove words that do not change the claim.

- State the intended meaning directly.

  - Remove obvious instructions and repeated task statements.

  - Remove unnecessary qualifications.

- Order claims by importance.

- Replace abstractions with observable actions, conditions, or outcomes.

- Add missing domain context. Omit explanation already established by context.

- Group related claims beneath a parent claim.

  - Put the decision or action in the parent.

  - Put reasons, conditions, steps, and outcomes in children.

---

## Deduction

_Derive consequences of the explanation._

- Predict how the revision changes the reader's interpretation, lookup, or decision.

---

## Induction

_Test those consequences and provisionally retain or revise the explanation._

- Apply the revision and test it against competing readings, factual constraints, and the reader's goal.

  - Correct unsupported absolutes and unnecessary hedges exposed by the check.

---

## Application

- Ask one question per turn when the reader must choose between tradeoffs.

# Conceptual Synthesis

- Search the domain and adjacent disciplines for existing concepts, terms, and models before inventing new ones.

---

## Artifact (Output)

_State the intended properties declaratively._

- **Lexicon:** Consistent names and definitions for necessary distinctions.

---

## Observation (Input)

_State the input categories and their roles._

- **Lexicon:** Existing names and definitions to reuse or revise.

- **Distinctions:** Ideas, relationships, or constraints that need consistent names.

- **Usage:** Examples and counterexamples that establish meaning.

---

## Abduction

_Devise an explanation for observations._

- Define the concept before naming it.

  - State its scope, exclusions, relationships, and necessary distinctions.

- Coin or revise a concise term that carries the distinction.

  - Propose how each relevant representation will use the term, its definition, and its constraints consistently.

---

## Deduction

_Derive consequences of the explanation._

- Predict how the concept classifies examples, counterexamples, and neighbouring concepts.

- Derive which meanings and distinctions must survive each proposed rename.

---

## Induction

_Test those consequences and provisionally retain or revise the explanation._

- Test the derived consequences against collected uses, counterexamples, and neighbouring concepts.

- Retain a concept only when it preserves a necessary distinction.

- Reuse retained concepts and terms across each relevant representation.

- Normalize meaningless variation and update or retire concepts when their underlying distinctions change.

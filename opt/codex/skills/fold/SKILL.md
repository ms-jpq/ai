# Fold

## Artifact (Output)

_State the intended properties declaratively._

- The structured representation incorporates the additions compactly, expressing repeated meaning once.

  - Required distinctions, ordering, scope, conditions, and uncertainty remain recoverable without unsupported claims.

- Unresolved questions, contradictions, and anything unabsorbed remain explicit.

- Removed material is fully represented in the revised structure.

---

## Observation (Input)

_State the input categories and their roles._

- Existing structured representation: the baseline to extend or reorganize while preserving its meaning.

- Accumulated material in scope: additions to incorporate, including their conditions and uncertainties.

---

## Abduction

_Devise an explanation for observations._

- Explain what the current organization or notation cannot express and propose a revision that expresses repeated meaning once.

  - Preserve differences in scope, conditions, and certainty.

---

## Deduction

_Derive consequences of the explanation._

- Map incoming claims to their proposed locations and identify affected existing claims.

- Predict which distinctions, ordering, and unresolved conflicts a reader could recover from the revision.

- Identify what the revision would absorb and what would remain.

---

## Induction

_Test those consequences and provisionally retain or revise the explanation._

- Apply the proposed changes and compare the result with both inputs.

  - Verify that the revision preserves meaning and uncertainty without adding unsupported claims.

  - Correct lost distinctions and unnecessary complexity.

- Remove only what is verified as represented in the revision. Retain the rest.

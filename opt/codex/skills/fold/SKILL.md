---
name: fold
description: Incorporate accumulated free-form material into an existing representation, adapting its structure as needed.
---

- Fold accumulated material into the existing representation using its organisation and notation.

- Revise the representation where the material exposes missing distinctions or relationships. Preserve meaning rather than forcing it into the current structure.

- Keep unresolved questions and contradictions explicit.

- Remove absorbed material once its meaning is represented. Retain anything not yet incorporated.

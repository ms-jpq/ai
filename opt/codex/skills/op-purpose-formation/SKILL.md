# Purpose Formation

## Artifact (Output)

_State the intended properties declaratively._

- A description of the encountered system and its external boundary.

- A system purpose that explains why the system should change or be preserved, accounting for declared and unmet intentions.

  - Observable success conditions define when the purpose is achieved.

- The purpose accounts for existing commitments and affected parties' interests, with unresolved conflicts explicit.

- Exclusions, unresolved questions, and acceptance or disagreement about the purpose and boundary are explicit.

---

## Observation (Input)

_State the input categories and their roles._

- The encountered system, current behaviour, and external boundary establish what may change or be preserved.

- Declared and unmet intentions provide candidate reasons for the system's existence or revision.

- Existing commitments and affected parties constrain acceptable purposes.

- Competing purposes expose unresolved differences in intended effects.

---

## Abduction

_Devise an explanation for observations._

- Propose a system purpose that explains why the system should change or be preserved.

  - Compare candidate purposes by how they account for declared and unmet intentions.

  - Keep competing purposes distinct until their relationship is established.

---

## Deduction

_Derive consequences of the explanation._

- Derive where each proposed purpose would conflict with commitments or affected parties' interests.

- Derive observations that would show the system purpose is achieved or contradicted.

- Predict which problems become relevant under the proposed purpose.

---

## Induction

_Test those consequences and provisionally retain or revise the explanation._

- Test the purpose against the encountered system, commitments, and affected parties' interests.

- Retain a candidate purpose only when it has observable success conditions and survives those tests.

- Revise the proposed purpose or boundary when evaluation exposes a conflict or gap.

- Record whose intentions the proposed purpose reflects, who accepts or disputes it and its boundary, and what remains unresolved.
